## 1. Overview

fund01 基金盯盘 (Fund01 Fund Tracker) is a browser extension that helps users track their fund (mutual fund / ETF) holdings, see intraday estimated NAV (net asset value) changes, monitor portfolio gains & losses, and follow market indices such as gold. It is a personal, non-commercial utility tool.

This policy explains what information the extension accesses, how it is used, and the choices available to you.

## 2. Data Collection

**We do not collect any personal data.** The extension does not collect, transmit, or store:

- Names, email addresses, or other personal identifiers
- Browsing history, bookmarks, or open tabs
- Cookies, sessions, or other tracking data
- Location data
- Behavioral or usage analytics

The developer has no servers and receives no data from the extension.

## 3. Local-Only Storage

All data created by the extension — your fund holdings, grouping and display settings, and cached market quotes — is stored **exclusively in your browser's local storage** (`chrome.storage.local` / `localStorage`). Nothing is uploaded to the developer or to any third party.

You can remove all stored data at any time by clearing the extension's data in your browser settings, or by uninstalling the extension. Uninstalling deletes all locally stored information.

## 4. Access to Public Market Data Providers

To provide fund valuation and market quotes, the extension sends requests to publicly available financial data endpoints (e.g., fund NAV and index quote APIs operated by public finance data providers such as Eastmoney, Sina Finance, Tencent, and fund123). These requests contain only necessary public identifiers — such as fund codes (e.g., "008987") — and never personal information. Responses are cached locally for display only; the traffic is not logged or reused for any other purpose.

## 5. Third-Party Sharing

We do not sell, rent, trade, or share any user data with third parties. The extension contains no advertising, no analytics SDKs, and no third-party code that collects data.

## 6. Data Retention & Deletion

Data remains on your device until you remove it. Deleting the extension's data in browser settings, or uninstalling the extension, permanently removes all locally stored information. The developer keeps no copies because no data ever reaches the developer.

## 7. Children's Privacy

The extension does not target children and does not knowingly collect any data from anyone, including children.
