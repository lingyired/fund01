# Single Purpose Statement — fund01 基金盯盘 (Fund01 Fund Tracker)

> 供 Chrome Web Store / Edge Add-ons 审核使用。三种粒度：一句话版、标准版（商店字段粘贴）、详细版（备用）。

---

## 一句话版（Short version）

Track your fund holdings — see real-time estimated NAV changes, gains & losses, and market context — all in one place.

## 标准版（Recommended, for the "Single Purpose" field）

**The single purpose of this extension is to help users track their fund holdings.**

It lets users add the funds they hold, displays each fund's intraday estimated NAV change (red for up, green for down), and summarizes the portfolio's daily and total gains & losses. To support this core purpose, it offers two auxiliary capabilities:

1. **Holding groups** — organize holdings into user-defined groups (e.g., by theme or strategy) for quicker overview. This is a way of organizing the same holdings, not a separate product feature.
2. **Market context** — show major indices and gold prices in the popup, giving fund investors market context that directly affects their fund decisions.

Everything in the extension serves this one purpose: helping the user monitor their own fund holdings. The extension does not provide trading, social features, news, or any unrelated functionality.

## 详细版（Detailed, for the detailed description section）

fund01 基金盯盘 has one clearly defined purpose: **fund holding tracking**. It is a personal "fund watchlist" tool that helps you monitor the money you already hold in funds.

- Its primary function is displaying your fund holdings with **intraday estimated NAV changes** and **portfolio gains & losses**, refreshed in the background.
- **Holding groups** help you organize those same holdings into categories — this is organization of the holdings, not an independent feature.
- **Index & gold quotes** in the popup provide market context around your fund holdings; they are read-only reference data shown alongside your holdings.

All features exist to support the single purpose above. There is no unrelated functionality: no social features, no e-commerce, no advertising, no news feed, no games.

## 中文对照（Chinese version, for your reference）

**单一用途：帮助用户跟踪自己的基金持仓。**

扩展让用户添加自己持有的基金，查看每只基金的盘中实时估值涨跌（红涨绿跌），并汇总整体当日与累计盈亏。围绕这一核心用途提供两个辅助能力：

1. **持仓分组**：将同一批持仓按用户自定义的分组归类，便于快速浏览——这是持仓的组织方式，不是独立功能。
2. **行情参考**：在弹窗中展示主要指数与黄金价格，为基金投资者提供与基金决策直接相关的市场背景。

扩展内所有功能都服务于「监控自己的基金持仓」这一个用途，不包含交易、社交、资讯、广告等任何无关功能。
